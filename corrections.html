Every figure we got wrong, and what it should have said.

When a number The North Hills Brief printed turns out to be wrong, it goes on this page: the figure, what we printed, what it should have been, and why. We don't fix numbers quietly. The next issue of the edition that carried it prints the correction too, and the issue as it was sent stays on file unchanged, with a corrected copy beside it.

Corrected September 25, 2026Beauty Pulse No. 03Website

Full-head color, floor median across the six towns

We printed$75
Correct$81

Our website's preview of Beauty Pulse No. 03 gave the median starting price for full-head color as $75 from September 20 to September 25, 2026. The correct figure is $81, across the 20 shops that post a full-head color price. The $75 came from an earlier count that had been replaced, and it survived on the site after the count changed.

Corrected September 17, 2026HomepagePLCB liquor licences

Active liquor licences in the six towns

We printed154
Correct129

From September 5 to September 17, 2026, our homepage said we tracked 154 active liquor licences across the six towns. We took the figure down on September 17 when we found the error. The correct count of licensed premises, from the Pennsylvania Liquor Control Board's records as of September 4, 2026, is 129. The 154 included 24 records that are not licensed premises: 16 special occasion permits, 7 grain alcohol permits and 1 transporter-for-hire licence. We now leave those three types out of every count.

The error was uneven by town, so any town-to-town comparison made from the old count was wrong too:
