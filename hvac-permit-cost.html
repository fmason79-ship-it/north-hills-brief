HVAC permit cost in the North Hills, six towns compared.

What it costs to pull a permit to replace a furnace or add central air in West View, Ross, Shaler, Ohio Township, McCandless and Franklin Park. Every fee below is read from the town's adopted fee schedule, and every schedule is named and dated. Where a town prints no fee, we say so rather than guess.

The six towns side by side.

Residential work only. Fees as of September 2026; we recheck every schedule before each monthly issue of HVAC Pulse.

Town by town.

The detail behind each row, and the record it comes from.

Ross Township

Ross prints the most detailed HVAC lines of the six towns. Residential fees, each per unit:

  • Replacement furnace: $75
  • Replacement A/C only: $75
  • New (add-on) A/C or heat pump: $75, with a plot plan
  • New furnace: $100
  • New furnace and new A/C together: $150
  • Pennsylvania UCC fee: $4.50

Where a plot plan applies, as it does for an add-on outdoor unit, the township also charges a $50 zoning fee. Replacing a furnace and an A/C at the same time is two replacement lines, $75 and $75. Ross's building code agency is Code.sys, which also serves West View.

Source: Ross Township 2026 Fee Schedule

Shaler Township

Shaler requires a permit only for work that "changes the original design." Check that rule before a like-for-like swap. Adding central air where there was none is priced as below.

  • Building permit: $120, plus $7 per $1,000 of cost
  • Labor and Industry (state) fee: $4.50
  • Contractor registration: $40 a year, expiring December 31

Source: Shaler Township Resolution 17-2026, effective July 1, 2026 · Shaler Building & Zoning

Town of McCandless

The town's current fee schedule prints no mechanical or electrical permit line, so there is no published price for a furnace or A/C permit. That is not the same as no permit. Call the town before you price a job, and do not quote a swap from the schedule.

Source: Town of McCandless Resolution 2025-32, adopted December 15, 2025

Ohio Township

Ohio Township's adopted fee schedule prints no mechanical line either. The one HVAC-relevant number it does print is the re-inspection fee: $100 for each return visit.

Source: Ohio Township Schedule of Fees

Franklin Park Borough

Franklin Park has no separate mechanical line. HVAC equipment is priced inside the electrical permit table, by the unit's rating in horsepower, kilowatts or kVA:

  • Fractional up to 1: $20.40
  • 1 to 3: $22.80
  • 4 to 7: $32.40

Source: Franklin Park Borough Resolution 1405-2025 · Franklin Park forms and schedules

West View Borough

We asked the Borough in writing whether a residential HVAC job needs a permit. The Borough Manager answered:

"We have not required a permit for a new HVAC system in a residential home."Bruce Fromlak, West View Borough Manager, by email, September 17, 2026

Those are the Borough's words, and we print them as written. They say "new HVAC system," not "replacement." The Borough's permit report from Code.sys also shows an HVAC permit issued on July 20, 2026 for a new furnace and A/C install on Oakley Avenue. We have asked the Borough to clarify which jobs it permits.

Where a permit is pulled, West View's fee comes from its building code agreement with Code.sys:

  • $45 for the first $1,000 of the job's cost
  • $15 for each $1,000 after that
  • $125 minimum
  • Plus a 20% borough fee

Sources: Borough Manager's email of September 17, 2026, on file · West View Borough agreement with Code.sys, Exhibit A, executed January 2024 · Borough permit report, January 2024 to August 2026

What we're still asking.

Open questions, in writing to each town. When an answer comes back, this page changes and the change is dated.

  • West View: which residential HVAC jobs need a permit, and whether adding central air does.
  • McCandless and Ohio Township: how a furnace or A/C swap is priced when the schedule prints no mechanical line.
  • Franklin Park: whether its residential initial permit fee applies on top of the electrical table.

How we read the fees.

Every figure on this page comes from the town's adopted fee schedule or, for West View, from the Borough's own records and written answer. We don't average, estimate or fill a blank. A fee that isn't printed is marked as not printed. If you see a newer schedule than the one we cite, write to hello@northhillsbrief.com and we'll correct the page and say so.
