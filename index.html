Who's filing this

Written by someone who lives here.

— Fred Mason

West View, PA · Founder & sole reporter

I live in West View and spend my working life in financial-services operations — the part of the job that's about reading records closely and making sure the numbers hold up. North Hills Brief is that same discipline pointed at my own neighborhood.

Small businesses in the North Hills are easy to overlook — there's no dedicated local desk covering what happens in six different council chambers every month. I built this to close that gap, starting with real estate and beauty, with trades likely next.

It's a small operation by design: one person reading the primary source, every week, so you don't have to.
