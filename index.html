Written by someone who lives here.

Fred Mason

West View, PA. Founder and sole reporter.

I live in West View and spend my working life in financial-services operations, the part of the job that's about reading records closely and making sure the numbers hold up. The North Hills Brief is that same discipline pointed at my own neighborhood.

Small businesses in the North Hills are easy to overlook. There's no dedicated local desk covering what happens in six different council chambers every month. I built this to close that gap, starting with the houses that change hands and the shops on the main streets, with more trades to follow.

It's a small operation by design: one person reading the primary source, every week, so you don't have to. If I got something wrong, or you want something read, write to me at hello@northhillsbrief.com.

How a line gets into the Brief

This is the first item in this week's Docket, with the rule behind each part of it.

Scheduled Ross · Nov 16

2027 budget at the business meeting, Mon Nov 16

The draft on Monday's agenda carries the same general fund, $30,187,816, and the same tax increase, from 4 to 4.5 mills, as the one commissioners saw Nov 4. The township's budget timetable sets Nov 16 for a public hearing and Dec 7 for adoption by ordinance.

Source: Agenda and draft 2027 budget, Nov 16 business meeting packet

The label
It says what kind of record this is: scheduled, on the agenda, decided or recorded. An agenda prints as scheduled. Only the minutes print as decided.
The town and the date
Each town is counted on its own, and every item carries the date of the meeting or the filing.
The figures
Numbers are copied from the document itself, not from someone's account of it.
The source line
Every item names the document it came from. If it can't be cited, it doesn't run.

What we read

County deeds and house cards
Every recorded sale in the six towns, joined to the county's own record of the house: year built, heating, central air. A deed the county has already marked as a family transfer stays out.
Township fee schedules
Each town's adopted permit fees, cited by name and date. Where a town doesn't require the permit, we say so, and we say who told us.
State licenses
Salon licenses from the State Board of Cosmetology and liquor license filings from the PLCB, both checked every Friday against a running registry.
Shop menus
What salons actually post on their own price lists, read shop by shop, with the read date on every price.
Minutes, meeting packets and check registers
Read for what was filed and what was decided. The check registers inside the packets show where each town's money went.
Sheriff and estate filings
The county sheriff sale list and Register of Wills filings, reported by property address.

Our standards

  • Every figure carries its source, its place and its date.
  • We print addresses, businesses and public bodies. We don't print private people's names.
  • This is not news, and it is not advice.
  • When we get something wrong, we say what we printed, what is correct and why, with dates, on the Corrections page.
