What the salon down the road charges, and who just got licensed.

Beauty Pulse reads the posted price lists of salons in West View, Ross, Shaler, Ohio Township, McCandless and Franklin Park, tracks new salon licenses from the state board, and sets wages beside them. It shows you where your prices sit. It doesn't tell you what to charge.

What's in every issue.

One page, every two weeks, with a box at the end that says exactly how every number was counted.

01

What the shops charge

The lowest posted price for a women's cut and a full-head color at each salon, read from the shop's own menu, with the read date. One shop is never printed as a town's price.

02

Who got licensed

New salon licenses from the State Board of Cosmetology, checked every Friday against a running registry. A license prints only after its address is placed in the right town on the map, not by ZIP code.

03

What labor costs

Stylist wages from job postings and federal wage data, each labeled with the area it actually measures.

04

Being added

Barbershops, nail studios, waxing and massage, each as its own row. A barber's price is never averaged in with a salon's.
