What's selling in six North Hills towns, and for how much.

Real Estate Edition reads every recorded deed in West View, Ross, Shaler, Ohio Township, McCandless and Franklin Park, and prints the count and the median price town by town, every Monday. It's for the businesses that live on property changing hands: agents, lenders, title and inspection firms, movers and remodelers.

What's in every issue.

One page, under 500 words, with a box at the end that says exactly how every number was counted.

01

Sales by town

Recorded sales and the median price for each of the six towns, from county deeds. One town's number never stands in for another's.

02

The rate

The 30-year fixed, three weeks side by side, labeled national, because it is.

03

The sheriff list

Properties on the county sheriff sale list, by address, in the first issue of each month.

04

Use This Week

A short list of checks to run against your own business, each with the number behind it. We don't tell you what to charge.

05

Being added

Zoning decisions from approved minutes, liquor license filings, town spending on named streets and projects, and estate filings by property address. Each runs once its source is built and checked. We never print the names of private individuals.
