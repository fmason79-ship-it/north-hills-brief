Questions.

I subscribed to the Real Estate Edition or HVAC Pulse. What happens now?

Both are now Property Pulse. You receive it every month at the price you already pay until your renewal date.

Why monthly and not weekly?

Deeds are recorded weeks after a closing, and a closing follows a contract signed weeks before that. A monthly window is the shortest one that holds a full count in every town. Each issue says which month of decisions the sales reflect.

Does the county's record always show central air correctly?

No. The house card shows what the county assessor last recorded, and some towns don't require a permit when air is added, so a card can be years out of date. We print what the card says and say that it can be stale.

Do you print names?

No. Addresses, businesses and public bodies print. Buyers and sellers do not.

How is it delivered?

By email, the first Monday of every month. A written brief, not a dashboard or a PDF you have to log in to find.
