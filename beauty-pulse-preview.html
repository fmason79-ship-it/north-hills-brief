Twelve of the 24 cut floors sit between $45 and $55. Six of them sit at exactly $55. Color floors run from $30 to $200, more than six to one, with no band in between. West View's $20 floor is one shop, not a town price. Ohio Township has 1 salon with an active state license and Franklin Park has 4, counted for the first time.

Use this week

  1. Check your posted cut price against the $45 to $55 band. Twelve of 24 shops sit inside it.
  2. Compare your color floor to the local spread of $30 to $200. There is no going rate.
  3. Check whether you sit above $55. Six shops are at exactly $55, and the highest floor is $65.
  4. Compare any job offer you post to $25.88 an hour, the Pittsburgh, PA general-market average as of September 6.
How we counted. Prices come from each shop's own public menu, read September 10 to 17, 2026. A shop's floor is the lowest price it posts for that service, and most are starting-at prices. Color means one full-head, single-process color; one shop's price includes a blow-dry. An ungendered haircut price counts as the women's cut floor. Barbershops, children's salons and Hampton Township shops are left out. Ohio Township and Franklin Park prices are not collected yet; their salon counts are map finds matched to active state licenses, September 21 and 25. New salon licenses are held while the state register's town matching is checked. Last issue's 27 included Hampton; this issue's 24 covers the six towns only. Color is on this definition for the first time. Wages: Indeed, Pittsburgh, PA general market, read September 6, 2026.
